How to Play

The goal is to beat the PatchMatch model at top-3 accuracy! You have up to 3 guesses to find the correct Sentinel-2 match for the given PlanetScope query image out of the 12 candidates.

Click the PatchMatch Prediction button to see the model's rankings and similarity scores for each candidate.
